# Operator guide — NRU

Portal: no dedicated self-service e-procurement portal was located --
procurement guidance is published directly on the Ministry of
Finance's own website (`naurufinance.info/treasury-division/
procurement/`), regulated by Part 3A of the Public Finance (Control and
Management) Act 1997 and the Public Finance (Control and Management)
Regulations 2013. Any procurement operation valued over $3,000, or
funded at any value by a foreign government or international/regional
institution, must be conducted through one of the three currently
authorised procurement agents (Brisbane Procurement, Eigigu
Procurement, Nauru Post Office) -- do not artificially split a larger
operation into smaller pieces to avoid this (Act s.15E(4)).

Entity: a Certificate of Incorporation (Corporations Act 1972,
Registrar of Corporations) valid 12 months from incorporation and
renewable annually on lodgement of the annual return -- a director/
officer who permits trading on an EXPIRED certificate is personally
liable for debts incurred during that period. If trading under a
business name, also register that name (Business Names Registration
Act 2018) and hold a Business Licence (Business Licences Act 2017).
Beneficial Ownership Act 2017 disclosure applies to corporations,
partnerships, multi-person business names, and trusts. Tax
Identification Number (TIN) registration with the Nauru Revenue Office
is required for every employer/payer (Employment and Services Tax Act).

Human-gated filing only.
